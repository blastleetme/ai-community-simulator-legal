AI Community Simulator

Privacy Policy
Last updated: September 13, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Community Simulator handles information when you use the Discord application.

AI Community Simulator is designed to provide AI-powered assistance, community tools, interactive games and persistent virtual-world features for Discord communities.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features used by a Discord server or user, the application may process information such as:

  • Discord user ID;
  • Discord server (guild) ID;
  • Discord channel ID;
  • username or display name;
  • server configuration and preferences;
  • language preferences;
  • game profile and progression data;
  • experience, levels and virtual currency;
  • quests, factions, lore, NPC and world data;
  • support/ticket information when support features are used;
  • knowledge-base information configured by server administrators;
  • AI prompts and related conversation content when AI features are used;
  • technical information required to operate and secure the service.

3. Message Content

AI Community Simulator may process message content when a feature specifically requires it, such as when a user directly asks the AI assistant a question or when a server feature explicitly uses message content.

The application does not need to send every Discord message to an AI provider. AI processing is intended to occur only when required by the relevant feature or configuration.

4. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • operate the Discord application;
  • provide AI assistance and responses;
  • maintain game profiles and progression;
  • maintain persistent world, lore and NPC systems;
  • provide server-specific configuration and knowledge;
  • provide support and ticket functionality;
  • prevent abuse and protect the service;
  • debug errors and improve reliability;
  • maintain usage limits and prevent excessive automated requests.

5. AI Processing

When you use an AI feature, the relevant prompt or content may be sent to an external AI service provider in order to generate a response.

AI Community Simulator may use OpenAI services for AI processing. Information sent for AI processing is handled according to the applicable policies and terms of the relevant provider.

Users should avoid submitting highly sensitive personal information to the AI assistant unless it is necessary and appropriate.

6. Storage

AI Community Simulator may store information required for the operation of its features, including server configuration, profiles, game progression, world data, knowledge and other application data.

The amount and duration of stored information may depend on the feature being used, server configuration and operational requirements.

7. AI Response Caching

To improve performance and reduce unnecessary AI requests, the application may temporarily cache certain AI responses.

Cached information may be automatically removed or replaced according to the application's configured cache period.

8. Data Sharing

AI Community Simulator does not sell personal information to advertisers.

Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the application, including infrastructure, hosting, database or AI service providers.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, legal process, security requirements or to protect the rights and safety of users and the service.

9. Discord

AI Community Simulator operates through Discord. Discord independently processes information according to its own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

AI Community Simulator is not operated by or affiliated with Discord Inc.

10. Server Administrators

Discord server administrators control how many AI Community Simulator features are configured and used in their server.

Server administrators may configure server-specific features such as language, knowledge, support systems and game functionality.

11. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures may be used to protect information processed by the application.

However, no internet service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

12. Data Deletion and Privacy Requests

Users may request information about data associated with their use of AI Community Simulator or request deletion where applicable.

Privacy and deletion requests should be submitted through the official support/community server associated with AI Community Simulator.

Some information may need to be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, dispute resolution or legitimate operational purposes.

13. Children's Privacy

AI Community Simulator is intended to be used through Discord and does not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children. Users must comply with Discord's applicable age requirements and policies.

14. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application, its features, data practices or applicable requirements change.

The latest version will always be published on this page.

15. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests or other concerns, please use the official support/community server associated with AI Community Simulator.
